Starting a notes blog

I’ll use this space to jot down short physics insights, derivations I find elegant, and notes from courses I teach or take. Expect topics around condensed matter physics, many-body theory, electron–phonon coupling, and the computational methods I use day to day.

Equations render with LaTeX, so I can write things inline like the acoustic phonon dispersion $\omega_{\mathbf q} = c_s\,|\mathbf q|$, or set them off as display math — for instance the Kubo formula for the conductivity,

$$ \sigma_{\alpha\beta}(\omega) = \frac{1}{\hbar V}\int_0^{\infty}\!\! dt\, e^{i\omega t}\!\int_0^{\beta}\!\! d\lambda\,\big\langle J_\beta(0)\,J_\alpha(t + i\hbar\lambda)\big\rangle . $$
